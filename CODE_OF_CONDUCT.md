# Code of conduct

Be professional and kind. Concretely:

- Assume competence and good faith — everybody here is donating time.
- Critique code, designs and measurements, never people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Keep discussions on-topic; take heat to a private channel.

Maintainers may delete comments or block participants who violate this after
one warning. Report problems by opening a private security advisory or
contacting a maintainer directly.
